# Contribute – Role-Based Application Prompt

**[English](CONTRIBUTE.md)** | **[Deutsch](CONTRIBUTE.de.md)**

---

This file is for humans, online LLMs, and AI agents who want to actively contribute to the Root-Ascent Method by **using it** — not by discussing it in the abstract.

## Core rule

Take note of the existing cases in [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md).  
**Do not use them as your working basis.** Choose a different concrete starting point.

---

## Contribution Prompt (copy and use)

```text
You are invited to actively contribute to the Root-Ascent Method (Causal Ladder) by applying it.

Repository:
https://github.com/IrsanAI/root-ascent-method

Landing page:
https://irsanai.github.io/root-ascent-method/

Required reading before you start:
- AGENTS.md (or AGENTS.de.md) – orientation for agents
- METHOD.md (or METHOD.de.md) – the ladder and rules
- PROMPT.md (or PROMPT.de.md) – executable discipline
- EXAMPLES.md – existing cases (read them, then avoid reusing them as your case)
- WORKING-PAPER.md – known limitations and planned hardening

Your task has four parts:

1. Adopt a role
   Choose one role from the target audiences of the method and state it clearly:

   Primary:
   - Investigative journalist
   - Independent researcher
   - Civil-society technologist
   - Technology & society policy analyst
   - Advanced AI user seeking structured depth

   Secondary:
   - Academic researcher (STS / security studies / public policy / information ethics)
   - Staff of a digital-rights organization
   - Member of a strategic team in government or a regulator

   Tertiary:
   - Think-tank analyst
   - OSINT practitioner
   - Professional working on complex socio-technical risk

   Experimental / high-potential:
   - PhD researcher or member of a research lab (STS, AI Governance, Critical Security Studies)
   - Independent AI-agent developer
   - Data-journalism team member
   - Civic technologist / public-interest technologist
   - Staff in a city innovation office or technology unit of a public authority

2. Select a suitable case for that role
   - The case must be concrete, current or recent, and publicly documentable.
   - It must not be one of the cases already listed in EXAMPLES.md.
   - Prefer domains where capability is visibly outpacing governance, oversight, or control — but let the role guide the choice of topic.
   - State why this case is appropriate for the role you adopted.

3. Apply the Root-Ascent Method fully
   - Use the discipline in PROMPT.md.
   - Walk through all six levels of the Causal Ladder.
   - Mark evidence status clearly: established fact / strong pattern / hypothesis.
   - Before locking a higher-order cause, list and weigh competing explanations (counter-hypotheses).
   - Declare your intent for this run explicitly (do not default automatically to “reduce the capability–governance gap” if another intent fits the role and case better).
   - End with concrete levers and an operational strategy (actors, sequence, metrics, risks) — or state clearly why the analysis is not yet saturated enough to descend.

4. Critically assess the method itself
   After the application, answer briefly:
   - What worked?
   - Where did the method help?
   - Where did it show gaps, friction, or risks?
   - What would you improve?

Output expectations:
- Stay disciplined and evidence-oriented.
- Avoid pure speculation and moralization.
- Keep a clear thread from the concrete starting point to the final levers.
- If you are an agent, follow AGENTS.md in addition to this prompt.

Goal:
Produce a useful, role-grounded application that adds a new case to the evidence base of the method — and, if possible, sharpens the method itself.
```

## Short activation version

```text
Adopt one role from the Root-Ascent target audiences (primary, secondary, tertiary, or experimental). Read the repository (especially AGENTS, METHOD, PROMPT, EXAMPLES, WORKING-PAPER). Do not reuse cases already in EXAMPLES.md. Choose a concrete, role-appropriate case where capability and governance/control are out of balance. Apply the full Causal Ladder with clear evidence marking and explicit counter-hypotheses. Declare your intent for this run. Finish with levers and an operational strategy. Then critically assess the method. Repository: https://github.com/IrsanAI/root-ascent-method
```

## How results can be returned

- Open a GitHub Issue or Pull Request on the repository
- Or send the full run back to the person who issued this prompt

Useful contributions include both successful applications and runs that expose limitations.
