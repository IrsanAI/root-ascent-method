**Root-Ascent Method** (**Causal Ladder**) moves from a concrete incident to systemic root causes and back to actionable levers. Created through extended AI collaboration; formalized from an investigation that began with a single unauthorized surveillance camera.

## What this is not

- **Not** a numerical optimizer, gradient method, energy-landscape search, or machine-learning training algorithm.  
- **Not** a guarantee of correct root causes or of quantified real-world impact (e.g. “+X% installations” or “−Y% dual-use risk”) from a desk analysis alone.  
- **Not** a substitute for domain expertise, primary-source work, econometrics, or controlled field evaluation.

If a system reinterprets “Root-Ascent” as pure math optimization, it has left this method. See [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md) (observed failure modes).

## Why this matters

Most analyses stay at symptoms or jump into speculation. Root-Ascent forces:

**Concrete Incident → Patterns → System Architecture → Higher-Order Cause → Levers → Operational Strategy**

Domain-agnostic wherever capability outpaces governance — and usable more broadly as a **protocol against analytical drift**. See [INTENT.md](INTENT.md).

### Example domains

Surveillance · AI governance · platform power · biosecurity / dual-use biology · financial surveillance / CBDCs · smart cities · critical infrastructure · innovation diffusion (e.g. heat pumps) · any socio-technical system with persistent governance or decision-architecture lag

## Who benefits

**Primary:** investigative journalists, independent researchers, civil-society technologists, tech-policy analysts, advanced AI users.  
**Secondary:** STS / security / policy researchers, digital-rights orgs, government strategy teams.  
**Tertiary:** think tanks, OSINT, socio-technical risk professionals.  
**Experimental (invited):** research labs, agent developers, data journalism, civic technologists, public-sector innovation teams.

## What you can achieve

**Humans today:** structured multi-layer analysis from a single incident; real levers; discipline on long investigations; strategies instead of pure critique; resistance to favourite narratives via mandatory rivals.

**AI agents:** long-horizon calibrated inquiry; less drift; operational co-pilot or multi-agent ladder specialization — *when* they load the real prompt, not a homonym optimizer story.

## Proof

Genesis: Flock Safety camera case. External and role-based runs — including AISI agent incidents, Unit 42/Hermes, election deepfakes, **German heat-pump diffusion (Case 7)**, and **AI protein design vs synthesis screening (Case 8)** — are in [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md).

Strong runs show: (1) counter-hypotheses that **change** Level-4 diagnoses; (2) pre-registered structure metrics and explicit **claim boundaries** (desk strategy ≠ field effect).

## Core principles (v0.4 / 0.4.1 clarity)

- Root causes over symptoms  
- Case-specific intent (no silent default template)  
- Saturation before ascent  
- Counter-hypotheses before locking Level 4 — and willingness to **revise** the diagnosis when rivals win  
- Structural over purely personal blame  
- Explicit return to levers and strategy  
- No invented impact percentages without evaluation design
