# Intent Layers – Root-Ascent Method

This document makes the **intent architecture** of the repository explicit. It is part of the v0.4 hardening: treat intent as something that can itself be examined with the Causal Ladder.

## 1. Declared (operational) intent

Help intelligence (human or artificial) climb from noise and symptoms to structural causes — and climb back down to things that can actually be changed.

In many runs this appears as: understand and reduce the gap between **capability** and **governance / control**. That framing is often correct. It is **not** mandatory as the default for every case.

## 2. Case-specific intent (required per run)

Every application must state its own intent in 1–3 sentences at the start (see [PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md) v0.4).

Examples of legitimate case intents:

- Journalistic exposure of systemic gaps and levers for public actors  
- Developer controllability of agent stacks  
- Whether a capability should be deployed at all  
- Legitimacy, power distribution, distributional justice  
- Regulatory design options under uncertainty  

Calibrate the whole run against **this** declared intent, not against an unspoken template.

## 3. Higher (repository-level) intent

A broader reading of why this repository exists:

> Provide a reusable, criticizable, versionable **discipline** so that intelligence can stay oriented and action-capable under acceleration — a protocol against drift, not only a toolkit for governance-gap case studies.

Under this reading, Root-Ascent is a building block for **process alignment**: aligning how humans and agents inquire and decide, not only aligning model outputs to fixed values.

The operational intent (section 1) is not wrong; it is often a **symptom-level statement** of this higher intent. Surveillance cameras, agent incidents, and deepfakes remain the best training and proof grounds. The stake is larger: shared orientation under rising complexity and power.

## 4. Applying the method *to* intents and strategies

You may use the Causal Ladder on an intent, strategy, organization, or this repository itself.

Rules:

1. Say explicitly that the object of analysis is an intent/strategy/org (not a news incident).  
2. Treat that object as Level-1 “incident.”  
3. Still require saturation checks and counter-hypotheses before locking a higher-order cause.  
4. Descend to levers: how to restate, narrow, or operationalize the intent; what would falsify success.  

Example questions:

- Is the stated goal a symptom of a deeper aim?  
- Which incentives make this intent hard to hold?  
- What would count as evidence that we are still on the wrong altitude of intent?  

## 5. Multi-role intent (optional protocol)

Same concrete case, several roles from the target audiences, each with its **own** declared intent and levers. Compare:

- Where diagnoses converge  
- Where levers diverge (power & responsibility)  
- What no single role can change alone  

This optional protocol surfaces the “power and responsibility” dimension without replacing the causal ladder. See [CONTRIBUTE.md](CONTRIBUTE.md).

## 6. Anti-patterns

- Silent default to “Capability > Governance” every time  
- Intent so vague that calibration is impossible  
- Intent so moralized that evidence becomes optional  
- Raising intent altitude forever without returning to action
