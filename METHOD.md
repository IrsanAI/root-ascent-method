## Overview

The **Root-Ascent Method** (Causal Ladder) is a disciplined process for analyzing complex socio-technical problems. It moves from a concrete starting point to deeper structural causes and back down to actionable levers.

Created through extended AI conversations; formalized from an investigation that began with a single unauthorized surveillance camera.

## Why It Matters

Most analyses stay at symptoms or jump into speculation. Root-Ascent forces controlled ascent and deliberate return to action:

**Concrete Incident → Patterns → System Architecture → Higher-Order Cause → Levers → Operational Strategy**

Domain-agnostic wherever capability outpaces governance, oversight, or control — and usable more broadly as a protocol against analytical drift.

## The Causal Ladder

1. **Concrete Incident** – Specific event, article, statement, or observation  
2. **Patterns** – Recurring behaviors, anomalies, correlations  
3. **System Architecture** – Structures, incentives, control loops, missing sources of truth  
4. **Higher-Order Cause** – Condition that allows patterns to persist (candidate, not default: “Capability scales faster than Governance”)  
5. **Levers** – Realistic intervention points  
6. **Operational Strategy** – Actors, sequence, metrics, risks  

## Core Rules (v0.4)

- Do not stay at the symptom level.
- **Declare a case-specific intent** at the start of every run; do not auto-default to the capability–governance gap.
- Ascend only after a brief **saturation check** (claim, evidence, counter-evidence, residual uncertainty).
- Before locking Level 4: **list and weigh rival explanations** (counter-hypotheses); state what would falsify the condensed hypothesis.
- Prefer structural explanations over individual blame; do not erase responsibility when it is relevant.
- Calibrate continuously against the declared intent.
- Move into levers and operational strategy once the higher-order cause is responsibly condensed.
- Mark evidence status: fact / strong pattern / hypothesis.

## Operating Modes

**Seeded** – User supplies the starting point.  
**Autonomous** – System selects a current public case (prefer cases not already in [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md)).

## Who Benefits

Primary: investigative journalists, independent researchers, civil-society technologists, tech-policy analysts, advanced AI users.  
Secondary: STS / security / policy researchers, digital-rights orgs, government strategy teams.  
Tertiary: think tanks, OSINT, socio-technical risk professionals.  
Experimental: research labs, agent developers, data journalism, civic technologists — actively invited to stress-test.
